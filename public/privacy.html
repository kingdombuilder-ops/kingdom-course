Privacy

Last updated: May 13, 2026

The Kingdom Course is a Catholic spiritual formation web application. We respect that what brings a person to this kind of work is often quiet, sometimes raw, and always personal. This page explains plainly what we collect, what we do with it, and what is yours.

We collect as little as possible. We do not sell your data. We do not run third-party advertising trackers. We do not profile you for resale.

What we collect

When you create an account

To know you across visits and personalize your formation walk, we collect:

  • Your first name (used for the personal greeting)
  • Your email address (used to identify your account and, occasionally, to send service messages such as a verification code)
  • A free-text answer to "where you are starting from" (used to orient the journey to your situation; you may leave this blank)
  • The date you signed up

If you sign in with Google

Google sends us your name, email address, and (optionally) profile picture for the sole purpose of creating your account. We request only what Google's standard sign-in scopes provide. We do not access your contacts, calendar, Drive, or any other Google data.

Server logs

Like any website, our hosting provider records standard request logs (IP address, user agent, page requested, timestamp) for security and operational purposes. These are typically retained for a short period and are not joined to your account profile.

What stays on your device

Reflective practices such as journal entries in the Daily Examen or Lectio Divina are stored only in your browser's local memory and are not transmitted to our servers. Clearing your browser data clears these notes.

How we use it

We use the small amount of data we hold for one purpose: to let you walk the Course as a known person rather than as a stranger. That includes signing you in when you return, greeting you by name, and saving your progress.

We do not send marketing emails. If that ever changes, you will be asked to opt in explicitly.

Who handles your data

We use a small set of trusted vendors to run the service. Each handles only what they need to do their job:

  • Clerk · authentication provider; stores your account record (name, email, sign-in method) and handles password-free login.
  • Vercel · hosting provider; serves the website and records standard server logs.
  • Google · only when you choose to sign in with Google; sends us your name and email to create your account.

We do not use third-party analytics, advertising networks, or session-replay tools.

Your rights

Whatever applicable privacy law you fall under — British Columbia's PIPA and Canada's PIPEDA, the EU's GDPR, California's CCPA, or others — the following are true here regardless:

  • You can ask what we have on file for your account.
  • You can correct anything that is wrong.
  • You can delete your account and the personal data tied to it.
  • You can export a copy of your account data.

To exercise any of these, email hello@kingdomcourse.org. We aim to respond within seven days.

Children

The Kingdom Course is intended for adults. We do not knowingly collect personal data from anyone under 13 (or under 16 where local law sets the higher threshold). If you believe a minor has created an account, please write to us and we will delete it.

Security

Authentication is handled by Clerk and protected by industry-standard practices. All traffic to the site is encrypted in transit (HTTPS). No system is perfectly secure; if we ever discover a breach affecting your account, we will notify you as required by law and as a matter of basic respect.

Changes

If we make a material change to this policy, we will update the date above and, for changes that affect what data we collect or how we use it, notify account holders directly.

Contact

The Kingdom Course is operated from Sidney, British Columbia, Canada. Questions, requests, or concerns about privacy go to hello@kingdomcourse.org.
