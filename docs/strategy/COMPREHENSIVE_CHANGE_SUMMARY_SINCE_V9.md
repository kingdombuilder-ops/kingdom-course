## Part 1 — The Three Architectural Shifts

Three major architectural shifts have happened since V9 was finalized. Each cascades through every document in the project.

### Shift 1 — Five Houses (replacing Four Houses)

**What V9 says:** Four Houses — Light (Dominican / Aquinas), Fire (Carmelite / Teresa), Peace (Franciscan / Francis), Glory (Ignatian / Ignatius).

**What we now have:** **Five Houses** — Light, Fire, **Joy**, Glory, **Earth**.

The shift has two parts:

**(a) Peace renamed to Joy.** The fourth House is still Franciscan, still patroned by St. Francis of Assisi, still mapped to Book 4 (The Way of Peace) and Key 4 (REST/ABIDE). But the *display name* is now **House of Joy**, not House of Peace. The Franciscan charism is more accurately *joy* than *peace* — *Pax et Bonum* is "Peace and Good," but Francis preached joy as the saint's signature, and the user's revised vision recognizes joy as deeper than peace ("the joy set before him, he endured the cross"). The internal slug stays `peace` for backward compatibility with quiz weights and saints data; the display label is "Joy" everywhere.

**(b) Earth added as the fifth House.** The Benedictine tradition becomes the fifth House, mapped to Book 6 (Civilization) and Key 6 (BUILD). Patron: St. Benedict of Nursia. Charism: Work / *Ora et labora*. Faculty: Body / Stability / Hands. Color: deep earth brown (#5C4A2E). Tradition: Benedictine. The Earth House is grounded in the lay vocation of the worker, the parent, the maker, the host — daily life made holy through stability and the Rule.

**Why Earth-as-Benedictine fits BUILD:** Book 6 was already the Benedictine book in V9's framing. The monasteries that preserved literacy, founded universities, built hospitals, developed Western agriculture — that *is* the civilizational engine V9 names. Benedict's *Rule* is the architectural pattern of Catholic civilization-building. Earth grounds Light, Fire, Joy, Glory in the actual material world of work, home, and stable place.

**Theological warrant:** The Five Houses don't break the Patristic four-Gospels / four-living-creatures framing because Earth represents a different theological category. Light/Fire/Joy/Glory map to the four Gospel charisms (Matthew/John/Luke/Mark) and the four faculties of the soul (mind/heart/will/action). Earth represents the *integration through embodied stability* — the body grounding the four faculties, the daily Rule binding the virtues, the patronage of Benedict (Patriarch of the West) anchoring the architectural foundation of European Catholic civilization. Earth is structurally distinct from the four mendicant/missionary orders.

**Faculties of the soul updated:**

| House | Faculty (V10) | V9 Faculty |
|---|---|---|
| Light | Mind (intellect) | Mind (intellect) |
| Fire | Heart (love) | Heart (love) |
| Joy | Will / open hand | Will |
| Glory | Action (courage) | Action (courage) |
| Earth | Body / stability | — (new) |

**Cardinal virtues updated:** Light→Prudence, Joy→Justice, Glory→Fortitude, Fire→Temperance. **Earth integrates all four through the daily Rule** — work, hospitality, and stability that bind the virtues into the rhythm of an ordinary embodied life.

**Leadership styles updated:**
- Light leaders: clarity and teaching
- Fire leaders: love and presence
- Joy leaders: wonder and the open hand
- Glory leaders: vision and action
- **Earth leaders (new): stability and the Rule** — show up every day for thirty years, build institutions and homes that outlast them, trust grows around them through unbroken continuity. The abbots, the parents whose children become saints because the table was always set, the teachers nobody ever quit on. Risk: rigidity.

### Shift 2 — Digital-First Scaling Strategy

**What V9 says:** Kingdom Groups are the small-group backbone (4-12 people walking a book together over 6 weeks). Lead/co-lead a Kingdom Group is a *required* deployment at Keys 5, 6, 7. Phase Zero ships with a "Kingdom Group system." Pillar 5 (Agora) is built around Kingdom Groups as the primary vehicle.

**What we now have:** **Digital-first formation in the Duolingo / university-course self-study pattern, with viral organic sharing.** Kingdom Groups are reframed as an *emergent social pattern* — what naturally happens when saints walk the Course concurrently, in whatever form the friendship makes possible. Never a prerequisite. Never a required deployment. The product surfaces digital affordances (invite-a-friend, opt-in shared progress) that lower friction; in-person community emerges as the organic fruit of transformation rather than a structural prerequisite the product imposes.

**The user's framing principle:** *"The app should give tools to the Church's life, not add to it."* And: *"Digital first, when built well, organically leaks into physical sharing — because the formation is actually effecting change and worthy of sharing."*

**The scaling pattern:** Nobody starts a Spanish small group to learn Spanish via Duolingo. Nobody forms a Stanford small group to take a Coursera class. Both products are designed for solo study with optional social affordances that *support* the solo path rather than replace it. When the formation is genuinely transformative, sharing happens organically — text a friend, post to social, send the URL in a Reddit reply, mention it at coffee.

**The principle:** *Form follows the friendship.* When two or more saints walk concurrently, what they do together is whatever the friendship makes possible:
- Daily text thread between two friends sharing what hit them in today's reading
- Weekly call with someone walking ahead, behind, or beside
- Small group on a video call across cities
- Small group in a home if they live near each other
- Discipling friendship — coffee monthly, accountability simple

The form is *whatever the friendship asks*. The product never imposes structure.

**What changed in deployment requirements:**

| V9 | V10 |
|---|---|
| Key 5: "Lead or co-lead a Kingdom Group walking through Book 1 or Book 2" — required | "Walk it with whoever responds — by message, by call, in person if they are near, however the friendship is given" |
| Key 6: "Launch a Kingdom Group series in your parish" | "Build something. Found a Catholic initiative in your profession. Adopt a campus, a city block, a workplace as your field. Invite the people God has placed in your life to walk this with you." |
| Key 7: "Commission and lead a full Kingdom Group through an entire book" — required | "Commission and accompany others through an entire step of the course — in person if they are near, by call or message if they are not. Plant a Kingdom cell in a new context — parish, city, workplace, campus, online community." |

**What stayed:** All teaching about Catholic small communities, parish renewal, the historical Christian movements that grew through small groups (Sant'Egidio, Charismatic Renewal, base communities, Franciscans, Jesuits) — preserved as **catechesis**. The product simply doesn't *require* in-person small-group formation as a deployment gate.

**Practice #15 in the Field Guide** — full rewrite: from "How to Start and Lead a Kingdom Group" (90-minute home-meeting structure) to **"How to Walk the Kingdom with Others"** ("Whatever the friendship asks") with five modes (text thread, weekly call, video small group, in-home gathering, discipling friendship) and the closing principle: *"The in-person community is the fruit of the digital faithfulness. It does not need to be programmed; it grows on its own when the formation is real."*

### Shift 3 — Hero Eyebrow

**What V9 says:** Master tagline is *"The kingdom of heaven is at hand"* (Matthew 4:17).

**What we now have:** Web app's Hero eyebrow reads **"The Kingdom of Eternal Life"** — the first declaration the visitor sees, before the headline. Names what the kingdom *is* (eternally present, ontological) rather than asserting what the project will *do* (make it visible). Echoes through Hero body copy ("a kingdom of eternal life, given now and forever"). Mt 4:17 remains the movement-level tagline at higher framing levels.

This is a **layered framing**: V9's master tagline still works at the Master Strategy / movement level; the web Hero adopts an even more upstream framing ("The Kingdom of Eternal Life") for what the visitor sees first.
